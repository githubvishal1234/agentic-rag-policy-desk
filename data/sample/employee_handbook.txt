EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Leave Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

2. Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

3. Reimbursement Policy

Employees can submit eligible business expense claims within 30 days of the expense date.

4. Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
