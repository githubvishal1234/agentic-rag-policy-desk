COMPANY IT POLICY

1. Password Policy

Employees must use strong passwords and must not share passwords with other employees.

Passwords should be changed according to the company's security requirements.

2. Device Security

Company laptops must use approved security software.

Employees must report lost or stolen devices to the IT department immediately.

3. Remote Access

Employees accessing company systems remotely must use the approved VPN.

4. Data Protection

Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.
