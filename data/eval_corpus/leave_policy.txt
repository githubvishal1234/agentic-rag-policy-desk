ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - LEAVE POLICY
Owned by People Operations

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire if not used by 31 March.

2. Requesting Leave

Leave is requested in the Harbor HR portal. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance. Managers should respond to requests within 5 working days.

3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

4. Parental Leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and the secondary caregiver to 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken at any point within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

5. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for other relatives.

6. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be granted with the approval of a director. Benefits continue during unpaid leave, but leave entitlement does not accrue.
