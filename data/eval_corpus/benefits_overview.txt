ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - BENEFITS OVERVIEW

1. Health Insurance

Medical insurance starts on the first day of employment and covers the employee, a spouse or partner, and children. Dental and vision cover begin after 90 days.

2. Retirement Plan

Aldermere matches retirement contributions up to 6% of base salary. The company match vests over 3 years: one third after each full year of service.

3. Learning Budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, conferences, books and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over.

4. Wellness Allowance

A wellness allowance of $600 per year can be spent on gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment or mental health apps.

5. Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free, confidential counselling 24 hours a day, with up to 8 sessions per issue per year, for employees and their household.

6. Commuter Benefit

Public transport passes can be paid from pre-tax salary through the commuter benefit, up to $300 per month.
