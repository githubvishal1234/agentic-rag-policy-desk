ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - NEW STARTER GUIDE

1. Your First Day

Please arrive at 8:30 on your first day and go to reception to collect your access badge. Your laptop will be ready at the IT Bar on the second floor, where the team will help you set up MFA in the Keyguard app.

2. Your Buddy

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Your buddy is your go-to person for informal questions about how things work.

3. Mandatory Training

Security awareness training and code of conduct training must be completed within your first 30 days. Data protection training follows within 60 days.

4. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months, with check-in reviews at 3 months and at 6 months. During probation, the notice period is 2 weeks on either side.

5. Useful Systems

Harbor is the HR portal for leave and personal details, Ledgerly handles expenses, Wayfare is used for travel booking and Deskly is used to book desks in the office.
