ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - PERFORMANCE AND PROMOTION POLICY

1. Review Cycle

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Employees write a self-assessment, and managers gather feedback from at least two peers.

2. Ratings

Ratings use a five-point scale from 1 (does not meet expectations) to 5 (exceptional). Ratings are calibrated across teams before they are shared with employees.

3. Promotions

Promotions are decided once a year, during the October cycle. An employee must normally have been in their current level for at least 12 months to be considered.

4. Salary Reviews

Salary increases take effect on 1 January and are based on the October rating and market data.

5. Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated 1 is placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP) lasting 60 days, with fortnightly check-ins with their manager.
