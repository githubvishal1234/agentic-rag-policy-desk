ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - CODE OF CONDUCT

1. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts and hospitality worth up to $100 from a business partner. Anything worth more must be declared on form ETH-07 within 7 days and may need to be returned. Cash and gift cards may never be accepted, whatever the value.

2. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal relationship or financial interest that could influence their work decisions. Disclosures are renewed every year in January.

3. Outside Employment

Second jobs and paid consulting are allowed only with written approval from the employee's manager and People Operations, and must not compete with Aldermere products.

4. Respect at Work

Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with People Operations, or through the Speak Up line.

5. Speak Up Line

The Speak Up line is run by an independent provider and accepts anonymous reports 24 hours a day, by phone on extension 5050 or online. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.
