ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - DATA RETENTION POLICY

1. Purpose

Information must be kept only as long as it is needed for business or legal reasons, and then destroyed securely.

2. Retention Periods

Customer contracts are kept for 7 years after the contract ends. Invoices and accounting records are kept for 10 years. Records of unsuccessful job applicants are deleted 6 months after the hiring decision.

Emails are automatically deleted after 3 years unless they are filed to a project folder. System and security logs are retained for 13 months.

3. Legal Hold

When Legal issues a legal hold notice, all related records must be preserved, even if their retention period has ended. Deleting records under a legal hold is a serious disciplinary offence.

4. Secure Disposal

Paper documents are destroyed in the locked shredding bins on every floor. Old laptops and drives are returned to IT, which wipes and certifies them before disposal.

5. Storage Locations

Confidential records are stored in the approved document management system. Restricted records must be stored in Vault, as required by the Information Security Policy.
