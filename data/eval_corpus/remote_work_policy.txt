ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - HYBRID AND REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. Hybrid Schedule

Most roles are hybrid. Employees are expected in the office three days per week, and Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days when the whole team should be on site. The third day is agreed with the manager.

2. Fully Remote Roles

A fully remote arrangement requires approval from a vice president and is reviewed every 12 months.

3. Home Office Support

Hybrid and remote employees can claim a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair or monitor, and a monthly internet allowance of $30. Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee.

4. Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year. A Remote Work Abroad Request (form HR-22) must be approved at least 4 weeks before travel, because of tax and immigration rules. Working from a country on the restricted list is never allowed.

5. Availability

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 in their home time zone, and must keep their calendar up to date.
