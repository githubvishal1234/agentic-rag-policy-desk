ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Submitting Expenses

Expenses are submitted in the Ledgerly expense app within 45 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted later are rejected unless the finance director grants an exception. Itemised receipts are required for any expense over $25.

2. Expense Categories

Each claim must use the correct category code: EXP-TRV for travel, EXP-MEAL for meals, EXP-SW for software subscriptions and EXP-TRN for training and courses. Using the wrong code delays reimbursement.

3. Approval Limits

Claims up to $2,500 are approved by the employee's manager. Claims above $2,500 also require approval from a director.

4. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year are issued a corporate card. Personal purchases on the corporate card are not allowed, and the card statement must be reconciled in Ledgerly every month.

5. Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except during client entertainment, where up to $60 per person is allowed. Traffic and parking fines, upgrades bought for personal comfort, and lost luggage fees are never reimbursed.

6. Mileage

Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and the office is not business mileage.
