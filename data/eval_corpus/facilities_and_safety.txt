ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - OFFICE FACILITIES AND SAFETY

1. Building Access

The office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Access outside these hours, or at weekends, requires a level B badge, which can be requested from Facilities with manager approval.

2. Desk Booking

Desks are booked in the Deskly app up to 2 weeks in advance. Desks not checked into by 10:00 are released automatically.

3. Visitors

Visitors must be registered in Deskly at least 24 hours before they arrive and must be accompanied by their host at all times.

4. Fire Safety

When the fire alarm sounds, leave by the nearest stairway; lifts must not be used. The assembly point is the north car park. Fire wardens wear yellow vests and take a roll call at the assembly point.

5. First Aid and Accidents

First aid kits are kept next to every kitchen. Any accident or injury at work, however minor, must be logged in the incident form on the intranet within 48 hours.

6. Parking

A limited number of parking permits are allocated each quarter by lottery. Electric vehicle chargers are free to use for up to 4 hours.
