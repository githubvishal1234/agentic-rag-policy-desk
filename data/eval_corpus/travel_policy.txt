ALDERMERE SYSTEMS - BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

1. Booking

All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the Wayfare travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made outside Wayfare are not covered by company travel insurance.

2. Flights

Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is allowed only for flights over 10 hours and requires director approval.

3. Hotels

Nightly hotel rates are capped by city tier: $280 in Tier 1 cities such as New York, London and Singapore, and $190 everywhere else. Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel earns a $50 per night allowance.

4. Meals and Per Diem

Instead of meal receipts, travellers receive a daily per diem of $75 for domestic trips and $95 for international trips. The per diem is reduced by 50% on days when meals are provided by a conference or client.

5. Ground Transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for travel to and from airports and client sites. Rental cars require manager approval and must be the compact category unless four or more employees travel together.
